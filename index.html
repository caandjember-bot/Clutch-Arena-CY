The arena

A LAN built like a main-stage esport, tuned for the first Clutch Arena chapter.

Clutch Arena brings the tension of Apex Legends into a live Cyprus setting: transparent competition, dedicated operations, branded broadcast coverage, and a clear bridge between amateur players and the international competitive scene.

This first edition is designed as the foundation for a regular Cyprus tournament circuit that can expand across other competitive games.

Tournament format

A clear path from registration to match point.

Request rulebook
01

Qualifications

Open registration with a minimum target of 531 participants, leading to 54 qualified teams.

02

Group phase

6 groups of 10 teams, live points, live standings, and the top 3 teams from each group advancing.

03

Playoffs

Top 20 teams enter a best-of-3 playoff bracket, including semi-finals before the final stage.

04

Grand final

Top 10 teams play on the main stage with a match-point final and awards ceremony.
